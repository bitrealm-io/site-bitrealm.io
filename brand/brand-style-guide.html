01 · IDEA AND WORDMARK

Dark, monospaced, one accent.

The brand reads like a terminal in a dark room: black ground, pale mint text, and a single coral accent that marks the one thing to act on. It looks like what the work is: software, measured and unhurried. No gradients on text, no photos of people, no second accent colour.

Wordmark

BITREALM
BITREALM
BITREALM

Mark. A coral square between two coral square brackets, on a 24-unit grid. The brackets run from y 2 to y 22, 3 units thick, with 5-unit arms, at x 3 and x 21. The square is 5 by 5, centred at (12,12). One bit, held in brackets.

Word. BITREALM in IBM Plex Mono Regular, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.06em. The mark sits on the cap height, gap of 0.4em. Write “Bitrealm LLC” in running text; use the uppercase form only as the wordmark.

Tagline. “Software that ships.” Sentence case, with the full stop, in Mint. It may sit under the wordmark or stand alone as a heading.

Clear space. Keep one mark-width free on all sides. Minimum height: 16px on screen, 5mm in print.

Colour. Coral mark with mint word on black is the primary lockup. On a mint or coral ground the whole mark goes black. Never white on coral (2.9:1, fails contrast) and never a coral word.

03 · TYPOGRAPHY

IBM Plex Mono, and only that.

One family at two weights. Regular (400) for everything; Medium (500) only for the display heading, headline numbers, and bold runs inside a paragraph. Open Font License, free for commercial use, on Google Fonts. Self-host the two woff2 files; do not load from Google at runtime. Fallback stack: ui-monospace, "SFMono-Regular", Menlo, Consolas, monospace.

Rules

Sentence case everywhere except the display heading and the wordmark. No italics (the family is loaded without them). No letter-spacing on body text. Measure 60 to 70 characters. Headings never wrap past four lines; cut words before shrinking the size. The justified display heading is a deliberate quirk of the brand: keep text-align: justify with text-align-last: left, and accept the gaps. Sizes above are screen sizes; samples on this page are scaled to fit.

05 · IMAGERY AND THE HERO

A point cloud over the depth gradient.

Software
that

What it is. Small near-white dots (#EEF4FF, 25 to 100 percent opacity, radius 0.8 to 2.2px on a 1920 grid) in three layers: a sparse field, a few dense clusters, and dotted lines running vertically and along shallow diagonals. It sits on the depth gradient, black at the top fading to pale slate at the foot.

Why. Structure emerging from points. It is also the one place the brand allows something other than flat colour.

Rules. Text goes over the black upper half only, in Mint. Never put a card or body paragraph on it. The graphic is generated from a deterministic seed and can be regenerated for other crops; do not replace it with stock footage or a photo. Motion is optional: if animated, drift the dots slowly, no flashes, respect prefers-reduced-motion.

Licence note. The graphic is an original asset and is safe to reuse anywhere.

06 · VOICE AND TOKENS

Say what the work is. Stop.

Short sentences. Plain nouns. Numbers where they exist, none invented. Write “Bitrealm LLC” or “the company” for the entity; no “I”, and no “we”. No adjectives that cannot be checked (“cutting-edge”, “world-class”). The reader wants to know what the company is, what it publishes, and how to reach it; tell them that.
